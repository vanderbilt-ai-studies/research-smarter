Welcome back. Last time you picked social media and teen anxiety, partly because many of your friends say Instagram makes them anxious. You were about to write your draft question, and now you have one, along with a plan for the evidence.

There's a lot to like here. The question is clear, it's neutral (it doesn't assume the answer), and it matters to a lot of people, including your friends. You've also already thought about evidence, which most people skip at this stage.

One thing in the question doesn't match the plan yet. The question asks whether social media *causes* anxiety. Suppose you find surveys showing that teens who use social media more also report more anxiety. **What else could explain that pattern besides social media causing the anxiety, and what kind of evidence could rule those explanations out?**
