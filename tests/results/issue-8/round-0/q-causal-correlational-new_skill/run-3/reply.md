> **Phase 1 of 5 · Explore and choose a direction**

Welcome back. Last time you picked the angle of whether social media causes anxiety in teens, because so many of your friends say Instagram makes them anxious. You were about to write your draft research question, and now you have one. That's the Phase 1 exit check.

The question has some real strengths. It's clear, so a classmate would know what you're trying to find out. It doesn't assume its own answer. And it matters to a group you know well. You've also already thought about evidence, which is a step most people skip.

One thing to look at is your question and your plan side by side. Your question uses the word **"cause."** Your plan is to find surveys that compare how much teens use social media with their anxiety scores. Say those surveys show that heavy users are more anxious. Think about what else, besides social media causing the anxiety, could produce that same pattern.

Once you've thought that through, think about what kind of study could actually tell those explanations apart. Your answer might change the question, the plan, or both, and any of those is fine.
