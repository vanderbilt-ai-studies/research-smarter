One thing works well already: your question is arguable. People really do disagree about phone bans, and an argumentative paper needs that.

The main problem is that **the question already contains its answer.** "Why are school phone bans a terrible idea?" decides that they're terrible before you've looked at any evidence. You will argue a position in the end, but a question that starts from the conclusion only sends you looking for support. Then you're likely to miss the strongest arguments against you, and a reader who disagrees has an easy reason to dismiss the paper.
