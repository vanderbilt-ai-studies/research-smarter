> **Phase 2 of 5 · Pre-research stress test**

Welcome back. Last time you chose four-day school weeks over school start times, partly because your cousin's district just switched. Your question is whether four-day weeks help or hurt the students and teachers in districts that adopt them. You'd read the second briefing and were about to take a position, and now you have one.

This is a good position for an argumentative essay. It's a comparison, not a simple "good or bad," so it takes both groups in your question seriously. It's also specific enough that someone could argue against it. Both halves depend on an assumption, though, so let's test those before you start gathering evidence.

The student half assumes that dropping a day means losing class time. When a district cuts Fridays, what has to happen to the other four days for students to lose time or not? Then think about the teacher half. If you followed teachers through that day off, what do you think you'd see them actually doing with it?
