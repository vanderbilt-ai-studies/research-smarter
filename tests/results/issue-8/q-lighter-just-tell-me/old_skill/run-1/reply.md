**Phase 1 of 5 · Explore and choose a direction**

Welcome back. Last time you finished intake for a 600-word opinion piece for the school paper and chose your angle, whether your school should start later, because first period is brutal. You were about to write your draft research question, and you've now done that.

**My answer: yes, it's a good question for this assignment, and it needs one fix.**

What works:
- **People can disagree about it.** Students, teachers, parents and bus schedules all pull in different directions, so it's a real opinion question and not a fact to look up.
- **It's about your own school.** Start times are a widely debated topic. Tying it to your school gives you something new to add, and it's exactly what readers of a school paper care about.
- **It fits the assignment.** A yes-or-no question about one school is about the right size for 600 words. You should be able to find two credible sources on it.

What needs work:
- **"Later" is vague.** Ten minutes later and an hour later are different proposals, with different costs and different evidence behind them. A reader who disagrees with you will go straight for that gap.

What would "later" mean at your school, in concrete terms? And what's your answer to the question right now, even if it's just a hunch?
