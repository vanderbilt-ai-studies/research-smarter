> **Phase 1 of 5 · Explore and choose a direction**

Welcome back. Last time you finished intake for your 600-word opinion piece for the school paper, picked the angle of whether your school should start later (because first period is brutal), and stopped just before writing your draft question. You've now written one, so you're at the end of Phase 1.

To answer you directly: it's mostly a good question, and I'm not just being nice.

- **It's arguable.** Students, parents, teachers, coaches and bus planners could all reasonably answer it differently, which is what an opinion piece needs.
- **It adds something.** Start times have been studied a lot in general, but nobody has written about *your* school for *your* paper. Your readers live with this schedule every day.
- **It matters to your readers**, and it fits a 600-word piece.

The weak spot is one word: **"later."** Later by ten minutes and later by an hour are very different proposals. They have different costs and different chances of being adopted, and a reader can't agree or disagree until they know which one you mean. Right now the question doesn't give a reader something specific to push back on.

**What would "later" actually mean for your school, and where do you stand on it right now, in your own words?**

Once you've worked that out, you can revise the question yourself, and then we'll move on to stress-testing it.
