**Phase 1 of 5 · Explore and choose a direction**

Welcome back. Last time you picked "whether my school should start later" for your 600-word opinion piece, and you chose it because first period is brutal. You're now at the last step of Phase 1, and you've just written your draft question, so we can check it.

It's a solid start, and here's why:
- **It's arguable.** People really do disagree about it. Students, parents, bus schedules, sports and teachers' commutes all pull in different directions, so it works for an opinion piece.
- **It's focused.** It's about *your* school, not schools in general. That's a good size for 600 words and gives you something to add that national articles don't.
- **It's neutral.** It doesn't assume the answer.
- **It matters.** The paper's readers live with that schedule every day.

There's one thing to look at. As written, someone could answer it with a single word, yes or no. Your piece will be stronger if the question points at what's actually being decided.

**To answer it well, what would you have to weigh against each other?** And while you think about it, which way do you lean right now?
