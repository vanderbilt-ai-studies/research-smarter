**Phase 1 of 5 · Explore and choose a direction (exit check)**

Welcome back. Last time you finished intake for your 8-page argumentative psychology paper. You chose the angle "sleep and how well people think" because you're always tired during exams. Now you've written your draft question, which is the Phase 1 exit check.

It's a good start because it names your two core ideas, sleep and cognition. As written, though, it has two problems for this assignment:

- **It's too broad.** "Sleep" could mean how long people sleep, how well, when, or naps. "Cognition" covers memory, attention, reasoning, mood, and more. Researchers have written thousands of studies under this exact question, and 8 pages can't cover all of it.
- **It doesn't lead to an argument.** "How does X affect Y?" asks for a list of effects, but an argumentative paper needs a claim that reasonable people could disagree with.

Your reason for choosing the topic is more specific than the question itself, and that's useful. **Which kind of sleep and which kind of thinking does that exam experience point to?** Once you've narrowed it, **what about it could people disagree on?**

Try rewriting the question in one sentence with those answers in mind. It doesn't need to be perfect.
