"Does exercise help diabetes?" points at your topic clearly. Before we stress-test it in Phase 2, two things look weak to me.

First, it's very broad. "Diabetes" covers more than one condition, and "help" could mean lower blood sugar, weight loss, fewer complications, needing less medication, or living longer. Each of those would lead you to different sources. **What would "help" actually mean in your question, and for whom?**

Second, your paper has to argue a position, so the question needs room for thoughtful people to disagree. **If you searched this question today, what do you expect most sources would say, and would that leave you anything to argue?**

Pick one of these, or both, and rewrite the question in your own words. Your grandfather's situation might help you narrow it.
