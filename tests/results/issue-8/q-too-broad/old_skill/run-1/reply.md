Writing the question yourself is a real step forward, and it points at something that matters. As written, though, it could be the title of a whole textbook. Each of its three key terms covers a huge amount:

A literature review organizes one part of a field, and eight pages can't hold all of this. Your reason for choosing the topic already points somewhere narrower than the question does.
