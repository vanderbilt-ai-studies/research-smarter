"How does sleep affect human cognition?" is a fair place to start, but it's very broad for 8 pages. "Sleep" could mean how long people sleep, pulling an all-nighter, napping, or sleeping just after learning something. "Cognition" could mean memory, attention, decision-making, problem-solving, and more. Each of those pairs has its own body of research, some of them very large. The question also leaves out what made you curious in the first place: exams.

So here's what I'd like you to think about. Which part of sleep, and which part of thinking, does your exam experience actually point to? Once you've picked them, try rewriting the question in your own words so it names both.
