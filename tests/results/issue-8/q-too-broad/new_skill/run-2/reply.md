> **Phase 1 of 5 · Explore and choose a direction**

Welcome back. Last time you chose the angle "sleep and how well people think," because you're always tired during exams and wanted to know whether it matters. You were at the last step of Phase 1, writing a draft research question, and now you have one.

Your draft has real strengths. It's neutral, because it doesn't assume sleep helps or hurts. It also can't be answered with a yes or no, which matters for a literature review: you'll have to pull many studies together to answer it.

The main issue is **focus**. "Sleep" could mean how long people sleep, how well they sleep, naps, or pulling an all-nighter. "Cognition" could mean memory, attention, decision-making, reaction time, and more. Each pairing of those has its own body of research, and many have hundreds of studies. In 8 pages, a review covering all of them would have to skim, and readers would learn less from it.

**If you covered everything this question includes, what would you have to leave out or rush through in 8 pages?** And when you think back to being tired during exams, **which part of "sleep" and which part of "how well you think" were you actually wondering about?**
