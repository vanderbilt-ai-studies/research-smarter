## Sources

**Peer-reviewed article.** Dyer FC, Dickinson JA. 1994. Development of sun compensation by honeybees: how partially experienced bees estimate the sun's course. Proc Natl Acad Sci U S A. 91(10):4471–4474. [PMC page](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC43807) · doi:10.1073/pnas.91.10.4471
The researchers raised bees that had only ever seen the late-afternoon sun. They then let them forage in the morning on an overcast day and recorded the direction of their dances. The authors conclude that bees seem to be born knowing the general pattern of the sun's movement, which lets them estimate where the sun is at times of day they've never seen it. *I could only read the abstract.*

**Peer-reviewed article.** Towne WF, Moscrip H. 2008. The connection between landscapes and the solar ephemeris in honeybees. J Exp Biol. 211(23):3729–3736. doi:10.1242/jeb.022970
The researchers moved hives between two matching landscapes that faced different directions. The bees kept dancing according to how the sun's course lined up with their *home* landscape. The authors suggest bees learn the sun's path relative to the whole horizon, possibly the skyline. *The journal blocked access to the full text, so this summary comes from the abstract listed on Crossref. That's also why there's a DOI but no link.*

**Peer-reviewed article.** Cheeseman JF, Millar CD, Greggers U, Lehmann K, Pawley MDM, Gallistel CR, Warman GR, Menzel R. 2014. Way-finding in displaced clock-shifted bees proves bees use a cognitive map. Proc Natl Acad Sci U S A. 111(24):8949–8954. [PMC page](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4066533) · doi:10.1073/pnas.1408039111
The researchers used anesthesia to shift the bees' internal clocks, which throws off their sun compass. They then released the bees away from the hive. Without a prominent landmark, the bees first flew in the wrong direction but still got home about as fast as the control bees did. When a row of bushes was present, the early wrong-direction flights disappeared. Other researchers have disputed this paper's interpretation (I saw this in search results but didn't open those critiques). *I read the abstract and most of the results, but not the methods.*
