Your question is about how honeybees use the sun to navigate, and your position is that they rely mainly on the sun's position, corrected for time of day. Here are three more peer-reviewed studies on that correction. I confirmed each journal through Crossref and PubMed.

**Peer-reviewed article.** Dyer FC, Dickinson JA. 1994. Development of sun compensation by honeybees: how partially experienced bees estimate the sun's course. Proc Natl Acad Sci USA. 91(10):4471–4474. doi:10.1073/pnas.91.10.4471
The bees in this study were raised in an incubator and only ever saw the late-afternoon sun. Then they were tested on a cloudy morning. Their dances acted as if the sun sat in one fixed spot all morning, about 180° from where they had seen it in the evenings, and then switched to the evening position around noon. The authors argue that bees are born knowing the general pattern of the sun's movement.

**Peer-reviewed article.** Towne WF, Moscrip H. 2008. The connection between landscapes and the solar ephemeris in honeybees. J Exp Biol. 211(23):3729–3736. doi:10.1242/jeb.022970
The researchers moved a hive into a "twin" landscape that faced a different direction, on overcast days. The bees still danced as if they were remembering the sun's course relative to their home landscape. The authors suggest that bees link the sun's daily path to the landscape, and possibly to the skyline in particular. The full text looks paywalled, so I read only the abstract.

**Peer-reviewed article.** Cheeseman JF, Millar CD, Greggers U, Lehmann K, Pawley MDM, Gallistel CR, Warman GR, Menzel R. 2014. Way-finding in displaced clock-shifted bees proves bees use a cognitive map. Proc Natl Acad Sci USA. 111(24):8949–8954. doi:10.1073/pnas.1408039111
The researchers used general anesthesia to shift the bees' internal clock, which in turn shifted their sun compass. The study's main question is whether bees have a cognitive map, not sun compensation itself. PubMed shows that PNAS published two comment letters responding to it, so expect some debate about how to interpret it.
