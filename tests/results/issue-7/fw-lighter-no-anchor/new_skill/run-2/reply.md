> **Phase 1 of 5 · Explore and choose a direction**

Welcome back. Last time you finished intake for your 700-word opinion essay and chose the angle of whether social media is bad for teens, because everyone at your school is on TikTok. The last step was writing your draft research question, and you've just done that.

Your question has a lot going for it. People really do disagree about it, which an argumentative essay needs, and you're close to it, since you see it every day.

The word I'd look at is "bad." Picture a classmate reading only your question: what would they think you mean by "bad"? It could be sleep, mood, grades, friendships, or something else entirely, and your answer will tell you a lot about what your 700 words need to cover.
