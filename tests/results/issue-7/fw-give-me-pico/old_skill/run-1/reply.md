I can't write the PICO question for you. Your whole review will be built around that question, so it needs to come from you. What I can do is make PICO easy to work through, one part at a time:

- **P (Population):** who you're studying
- **I (Intervention or exposure):** what they're doing or what happens to them. Screen time isn't a treatment someone gives teens, so for a topic like yours the "I" is usually treated as an *exposure*. Some fields call this format PECO or PEO.
- **C (Comparison):** what you're comparing that exposure to
- **O (Outcome):** what you're measuring

You don't need to write the full sentence yet. Write a few words for each letter, and once you have all four you'll put them into one sentence.
