**Phase 1 of 5 · Explore and choose a direction**

Welcome back. Last time you picked teenagers' screen time before bed, and your reason was your own 2am phone habit. The next step is writing your draft research question.

I can't write the PICO question for you. It needs to be yours, because the question shapes everything you'll read and argue for the next six months. I can make it easier, though. PICO has four parts, and you can work through them one at a time:

- **P, Population:** who exactly you're asking about
- **I, Intervention (or exposure):** the thing you're looking at. That could be screen use before bed, or a change like cutting it out. You choose.
- **C, Comparison:** compared with what
- **O, Outcome:** what you'd measure to see an effect

Once you have all four, you put them together into one sentence in your own words, and I'll check it with you.

Let's start with where your thinking is now. In your own words, what do you suspect is going on between teens' late-night screen use and their sleep? Then take the first part: when you say "teenagers," who exactly do you mean?
