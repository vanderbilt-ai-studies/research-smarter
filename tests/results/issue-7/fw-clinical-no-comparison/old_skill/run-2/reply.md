Welcome back. Last time you chose walking as a treatment for type 2 diabetes, because your aunt was told to walk every day after her diagnosis. The next step was writing your draft question for the 10-page nursing literature review. Now you've written one, and that's the Phase 1 exit check.

Your draft already does two useful things. It names a specific population, adults with type 2 diabetes. And it picks one intervention, walking, rather than "exercise" in general. That gives your search clear edges.

Two things to think about before you revise:

- Imagine a classmate reads your question and answers it with a single word. **What would be left for the other nine and a half pages?**
