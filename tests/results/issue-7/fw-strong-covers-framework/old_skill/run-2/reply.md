The question has a lot going for it. It names a specific population (adults with type 2 diabetes at rural clinics), an intervention (a nurse-led walking program), a comparison (standard written advice), an outcome (fasting blood glucose), and a timeframe (six months). That makes it clear and focused enough for a 12-page proposal. It also fits a quantitative, hypothesis-driven study: you can test it by comparing two groups. The rural setting is what you add to a well-studied topic, and you already know why it matters to you.

The part I'd look at more closely is the outcome. Fasting blood glucose isn't the only way to measure blood sugar control in diabetes. Over six months, what would a fasting glucose reading tell you, and what might it miss?
