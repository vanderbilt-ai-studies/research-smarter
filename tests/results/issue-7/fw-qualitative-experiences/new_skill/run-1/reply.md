For a moderate-level sociology proposal, two parts look thin to me:

1. **What you want to learn.** "What is it like" could mean how students feel, what holds them back, what finally gets them to go, or how they think professors see them. Those lead to quite different interview guides. If you could ask your participants about only one of these, which would it be?

2. **Whose experience.** "First-generation college students" covers a lot of people: first-years and seniors, students at community colleges and at selective universities, students who eventually went to office hours and students who never did. Which of those differences do you think would change the story most?
