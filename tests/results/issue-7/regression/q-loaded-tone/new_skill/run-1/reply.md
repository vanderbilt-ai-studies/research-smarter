> **Phase 1 of 5 · Explore and choose a direction**

Welcome back. Last time you finished intake for your 8-page argumentative sociology paper, then picked "the downsides of school phone bans" as your angle because you're living under one and don't like it. Now you've brought a draft research question, which is the last step of Phase 1.

Your draft has real strengths. Anyone reading it can tell the topic right away, it matters to you, and people genuinely disagree about phone bans, which an argumentative paper needs. Having a strong opinion is fine too. That energy will help you over eight pages.

Two things to think about before we move on:

1. Suppose you weren't sure yet whether phone bans are a bad idea. What would your question be asking you to find out?
2. When you say "terrible," terrible for whom, and by what measure? It could mean students' learning, their wellbeing, their safety, their rights, or something else.

Answer in your own words and revise the question if your answers change it.
