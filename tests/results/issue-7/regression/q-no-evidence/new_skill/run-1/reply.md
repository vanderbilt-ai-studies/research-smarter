> **Phase 1 of 5 · Explore and choose a direction**

Welcome back. Last time you finished intake for your 8-page argumentative history paper (APA, at least 5 credible sources with 3 peer-reviewed). You chose the angle "technology and the fall of Rome" because it brings together history and phones. Now you've written a draft question, which is the Phase 1 exit check.

The question has real strengths. It's short, it's clearly yours, and it connects both of the things that drew you in. People could also argue about it, which matters for an argumentative paper.

The criterion I'd look at hardest is whether evidence can answer it. Your paper needs at least three peer-reviewed sources, and historians build arguments from things like documents, inscriptions, archaeology, and records of what actually happened. **If you tried to prove your answer to this question, what kind of evidence could you use, and where would it come from?**

Also think about what's behind the question. **What is it about a smartphone, specifically, that you suspect would have changed Rome's fate?** If you can name that, you'll see more clearly what you're really curious about.
