## Open issues these results raise

- Whether marching bands in four-day districts actually lost rehearsal time depends on when they rehearse. If they mostly rehearse after school, or the remaining school days got longer, the effect could differ from what you expected.
- The free fifth day shows up in several of these sources, and they describe it in different ways.
- Since there's no direct research, you'll need to decide what kind of evidence can carry your argument.
