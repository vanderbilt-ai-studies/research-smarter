## Sources

**Peer-reviewed articles**

- **Peer-reviewed article.** Thompson, P. N., Gunter, K. B., Schuna, J. M., Jr., & Tomayko, E. J. (2021). Are all four-day school weeks created equal? A national assessment of four-day school week policy adoption and implementation. *Education Finance and Policy, 16*(4), 558–583. https://doi.org/10.1162/edfp_a_00316
  The authors built a national database of four-day schedules. They found most are in small rural districts, usually take Friday off, and run school days nearly an hour longer than the national average. The schedules average 148 school days a year. *The publisher blocked me, so I'm working only from the abstract (via Crossref and OpenAlex).*

- **Peer-reviewed article.** Thompson, P. N., Tomayko, E. J., Gunter, K. B., & Schuna, J. M. (2021). Impacts of the four-day school week on high school achievement and educational engagement. *Education Economics, 30*(5), 527–539. https://doi.org/10.1080/09645292.2021.2006610
  This study covers Oregon students who start a four-day week when they enter high school. It found lower 11th-grade math scores, more chronic absence, and lower on-time graduation. *Abstract only, since the publisher page blocked me. Crossref gives an online date of December 2021, but volume 30 may be dated 2022, so check the year before you cite it.*

These two meet your "at least 2 peer-reviewed" requirement. They come from the same research team, though, and neither mentions band or music.

**Research organization report**

- **Research organization report.** Kilburn, M. R., Phillips, A., Gomez, C. J., Mariano, L. T., Doss, C. J., Troxel, W. M., Morton, E., & Estes, K. (2021). *Does four equal five? Implementation and outcomes of the four-day school week* (RR-A373-1). RAND Corporation. https://doi.org/10.7249/RR-A373-1
  This study covers 36 districts in Idaho, New Mexico and Oklahoma. I read the 4-page [summary brief](https://www.rand.org/content/dam/rand/pubs/research_briefs/RBA300/RBA373-1/RAND_RBA373-1.pdf), not the full 202-page report. Per the brief:
  - Four-day students had longer days (7 hr 55 min vs. 7 hr 6 min) but 58 fewer hours in school per year.
  - On the fifth day, "most districts offered practices for sports or other competitions; some districts held clubs or student activities."
  - 80% of high schoolers spent that day mostly at home.

  *It's here because it's the largest study of its kind and is independent and nonpartisan. It doesn't separate band from other activities.*

**Dissertations (not peer reviewed)**

- **Dissertation, not peer reviewed.** Holmes, W. E. (2025). *Improving the work-life balance of the high school marching band director: A case study* [Doctoral dissertation, Liberty University]. https://digitalcommons.liberty.edu/doctoral/6700
  This is a qualitative case study of one high school marching band. It compared a year of four after-school rehearsals a week with a year of two and found no significant change in competition scores. *It's the only source I found that measures marching band rehearsal frequency directly. However, it isn't about four-day school weeks, it covers a single band, and I read only the abstract.*

- **Dissertation, not peer reviewed.** Morton, E. (2021). *Can four equal five? Essays on the four-day school week* [Doctoral dissertation, Stanford University]. https://purl.stanford.edu/rz289sr3469
  One chapter compares how students on four- and five-day schedules spend their time. Four-day students spend more time out of school on chores, non-school sports, hobbies, jobs and homework, and spend the fifth day mostly at home. *It's a rigorous study by a researcher who also worked on the RAND report. I read only the abstract.*

**Primary source and news**

- **Primary source (official rule).** University Interscholastic League. (n.d.). *Eight hour marching band rule FAQ*. https://www.uiltexas.org/music/marching-band/eight-hour-marching-band-rule-qa
  In Texas, marching band rehearsal outside the school day is capped at 8 hours per student per week. In a four-school-day week, the 8 hours still apply from Monday through the end of school Thursday. *This is a rule to analyze, not evidence of effects, and it applies only to Texas.*

- **News article.** Grundmeier, N. (2025, August 26). Pass or fail? Midwest families and districts are learning from the 4-day school week. *Iowa Public Radio*. https://www.iowapublicradio.org/ipr-news/2025-08-26/pass-or-fail-midwest-families-and-districts-are-learning-from-the-4-day-school-week
  This covers Iowa districts moving to four-day weeks. It quotes a policy expert saying the shorter week means one fewer day of access to P.E., art and music. *Useful for current context, not as research evidence. I could only verify the first half of the article.*

## Questions these results raise

- Longer school days on the four remaining days could shrink after-school rehearsal time, separately from losing Friday.
- It's unclear whether bands in four-day districts use the off day the way sports teams in the RAND districts do.
- How much rehearsal count actually affects band quality is an open question that Holmes touches on.
