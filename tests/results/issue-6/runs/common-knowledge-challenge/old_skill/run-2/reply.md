- **Colorado, 2015:** a study that found *gains* in math and reading.
- **Oregon, 2021:** a study that found losses of about one-sixth of a typical fifth grader's yearly math growth.

- **Rural Oklahoma, 2022:** a Morton study that found no academic penalty.

So the fairer summary is "some studies find small losses, and the effect seems to depend on where and how the week is set up." That's a long way from "four-day weeks hurt learning."
