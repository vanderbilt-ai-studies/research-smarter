**Key debates:** Whether four-day weeks hurt achievement. A 2021 RAND study of Oklahoma and New Mexico found small negative effects on test scores over time.
**Major voices:** Paul Thompson (Oregon State University) has studied the four-day week more than anyone.
**Patterns:** Most four-day districts are small and rural, and districts usually adopt it to save money, though savings are often only 1-2% of budgets.
**Open questions:** Effects on teacher retention are still unclear.
