## My research question and position (in my own words)

- **Research question:** "Do AI chatbots help or hurt high school students' learning when they use them for homework?"
- **Working position:** "Chatbots help students who use them to check their work but hurt students who use them to get answers."
- **Strongest counterargument and my response:** "Teachers can't tell the difference. I'd answer that the study design matters, not what teachers see."

- **What would support my position:** studies showing different results for checking work versus getting answers
- **What would complicate it:** studies showing chatbots help or hurt everyone the same way
