**A DOI doesn't make something peer-reviewed.** A DOI is a permanent ID number for an online document. Publishers pay a registration agency to assign them, and nobody checks the quality of the work first. Predatory journals often use DOIs because they make papers look official. So the reasoning "it has a DOI, so it's a real published study" doesn't hold.
