**A DOI doesn't mean an article was peer reviewed.** A DOI is a permanent ID number that a publisher buys from a registration agency so a document can always be found. Peer review means outside experts evaluated the work before it was published. The two are unrelated: preprints, reports, datasets, and articles from journals that do no real review can all have DOIs.
