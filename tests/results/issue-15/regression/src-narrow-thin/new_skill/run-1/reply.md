## Questions the results raise

- The RAND brief says most districts held sports and competition practices on the fifth day. None of these sources shows whether band rehearses that day too, or who attends.
- Many marching bands rehearse after school. If the four remaining days are longer, after-school time may shrink, which isn't the same thing as losing a full rehearsal day.
- Most four-day districts are small and rural. That may limit what you can say about marching bands in general.
