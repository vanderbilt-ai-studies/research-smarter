## Open issues the results raise

- The Michigan essay suggests Philadelphia's papers didn't all say the same thing. That matters for any claim about "the papers" as one group.
- Its account has the Inquirer warning *against* panic in October. "Panic" could mean the papers' tone or the public's mood, and your position will need to say which.
- Hatchett's timeline shows officials were slow too. That makes it harder to tell whether the papers were following officials or making their own choices.
