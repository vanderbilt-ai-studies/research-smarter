- **It's secondhand.** The article is a journalist's report on a study. The scientists' quotes were picked by the journalist, and you can't see the study's methods, sample, or data. For a lab report, the study itself is the evidence.

The good news is that news articles about new findings almost always name or link the original study. That's usually the fastest way to find a peer-reviewed source.
